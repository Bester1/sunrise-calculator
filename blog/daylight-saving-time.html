When Does Daylight Saving Time End in 2026?

The clocks go back one hour on Sunday 25 October 2026 in the UK, Ireland and the European Union, and on Sunday 1 November 2026 in most of the United States and Canada. South Africa doesn't change its clocks.

Places that never change their clocks

South Africa (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban), most of Africa, Japan, China, India, Singapore and the UAE stay on one time all year. Mexico dropped daylight saving in 2022 (apart from areas along the US border), and Brazil dropped it in 2019.

What happens to sunrise and sunset

Daylight saving moves the clock, not the sun. On the day the clocks go back, sunrise and sunset both appear about an hour earlier: lighter mornings, darker evenings.

Is daylight saving time being abolished?

In the US, not yet. The House of Representatives passed the Sunshine Protection Act, which would make daylight saving time permanent, on 14 July 2026 by 308 votes to 117. The Senate hasn't voted on it, so it isn't law and the clocks still go back on 1 November.

In the EU, not yet either. The European Commission proposed ending the twice-yearly change in 2018, but member states have never agreed in the Council which time to keep. The Commission started a new study on the effects of clock changes in 2026. Until a law passes, the clocks go back on 25 October.
