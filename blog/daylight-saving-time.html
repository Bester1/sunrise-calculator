When Does Daylight Saving Time Start

Daylight saving is a clock change, not a change in when the sun rises. The sunrise pages on this site format the clock in each city's own zone, so a clock change shows up as a shifted local time.

These are the 2026 offset changes for the cities on this site, taken from the timezone data on this computer:

  • New York (America/New_York): 8 March 2026 at 03:00, 1 November 2026 at 02:00.
  • London (Europe/London): 29 March 2026 at 02:00, 25 October 2026 at 02:00.
  • Sydney (Australia/Sydney): 5 April 2026 at 03:00, 4 October 2026 at 03:00.
  • Paris (Europe/Paris): 29 March 2026 at 03:00, 25 October 2026 at 03:00.
  • Berlin (Europe/Berlin): 29 March 2026 at 03:00, 25 October 2026 at 03:00.

No offset change in 2026 for Cape Town, Johannesburg, Tokyo, Lagos, Dubai. Cape Town and Johannesburg stay on the same standard time all year.
